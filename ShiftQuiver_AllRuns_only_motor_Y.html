For each candidate point (every ½ die pitch), all arrows within the radius are tested. An arrow "points in" if its direction is within ± the angle tolerance of the direction to the point. A zone is marked when at least the chosen share of the counted arrows point in (default ¾; if more than ¼ point elsewhere it is rejected) and the inward arrows come from enough of the 8 directions around it (default 6; lower it to also catch convergence onto a line), so a one-sided flow is not counted. Short arrows (fraction of the 95th-percentile length) are noise and are not counted. Overlapping hits keep only the strongest. Persistence keeps only zones that recur at about the same place in recent scans (noise zones flicker). Label = share pointing in. Set persistence to 1 of 1 to see single-scan hits.

Slope = least-squares fit over the trailing window, per hour (from timestamps). Angle = slope as drawn on this chart, so it depends on the axis scaling. State: flat if the fitted change across the window is ≤ tolerance. A scan is stable if all of the next N values stay within ± tolerance of their mean. "Settled from" = the first scan after which every N-scan window is stable. Note: the N-window test cannot see drift slower than tol / (N·Δt); use the drift limit or "hold to end", and check the net-drift card.
